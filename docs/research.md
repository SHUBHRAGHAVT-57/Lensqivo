# Research and product direction

Research checked 4 November 2026. Competitor descriptions below are vendor documentation, not independent performance tests. The decision is to build an approachable creative camera, rather than start with a full cinema recorder.

## Premium capture iteration

The interaction research favors a substantial viewfinder, compact actionable controls and optional creative drawers. [Blackmagic Camera](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/blackmagiccamera) demonstrates contextual monitoring; [ProShot](https://www.riseupgames.com/proshot) emphasizes useful camera controls and manufacturer capability limits. Neither page establishes performance parity on unspecified hardware. [SODA's Android listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.snowcorp.soda.android) and [B612](https://b612.snow.me/) establish real-time beauty as a product category, not independent quality evidence. [VSCO Recipes](https://www.vsco.co/features/recipes) supports the value of repeatable creative settings; Lensqivo adds three local reusable slots rather than copying proprietary presets or assets.

Native still quality takes precedence over a universal shared-stream snapshot path. AndroidX's [StreamSharing source](https://github.com/androidx/androidx/blob/androidx-main/camera/camera-core/src/main/java/androidx/camera/core/streamsharing/StreamSharing.java) routes its ImageCapture to a shared-stream JPEG snapshot. Lensqivo now targets preview alone in photo mode and applies edits after native capture. This source explanation is from current AndroidX, not decompilation of the pinned CameraX binary. [ImageCapture configuration](https://developer.android.com/reference/androidx/camera/core/ImageCapture.Builder) documents resolution/capture choices; no guaranteed pixel count or vendor-stock quality is claimed.

The [pinned Media3 1.11.1 GlEffect contract](https://github.com/androidx/media/blob/1.11.1/libraries/effect/src/main/java/androidx/media3/effect/GlEffect.java) specifies linear BT.709 for SDR. A shared custom shader performs edge-aware skin-color-selective softening, gentle glow and original color matrices. JPEG tiles decode/encode sRGB around this linear working space. The shader does not detect faces. [MediaPipe Face Landmarker](https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/face_landmarker/android) could support a future localized mask, but adds a model, tracking and integration costs; it is not a ready-made beauty renderer.

[CameraX Extensions](https://developer.android.com/media/camera/camerax/extensions-api) are per-camera vendor capabilities. HDR/Night remain deferred until a native extension capture path is qualified; ordinary SDR capture is not relabeled HDR. ExifInterface [1.4.2 release guidance](https://developer.android.com/jetpack/androidx/releases/exifinterface) informs the metadata dependency. New settings expose only implemented flash, timer, mirroring, original preservation, grid, audio and reusable recipes.

## What informed the direction

| Evidence | What it supports | Lensqivo decision |
|---|---|---|
| [Blackmagic specifications](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/blackmagiccamera/techspecs) list monitoring/LUT tools and a particular Android hardware list, with Android 13+ and feature exceptions. No publication date shown. | Professional monitoring has value; broad hardware promises need qualification. | Do not promise Log, UHD or vendor-wide support. Make unsupported configurations explicit and keep original capture as a recovery route. |
| [ProShot Android guide](https://www.riseupgames.com/proshot/android) groups advanced controls and explains manufacturer restrictions on lenses, resolution and vendor extensions. No update date shown; its release-planning text may be stale. | Capability-aware UI and related controls grouped together. | Show exposure and zoom only when ranges exist; place adjustments on demand, retain a visible shutter. Manual sensor control is a later capability-gated feature. |
| [MotionCam](https://motioncamapp.com/) describes RAW/DirectLog recording and sensor monitoring. No publication date shown. | There is a specialist workflow beyond ordinary processed capture. It does not establish universal device performance. | Defer RAW video/Log: they would introduce a separate pipeline before the ordinary camera is proven. |
| [Open Camera help](https://opencamera.org.uk/help.html) and [device notes](https://opencamera.org.uk/devices.html) describe feature dependencies and vendor Camera2 behavior. No clear update date shown. | Device support cannot be inferred solely from API availability or a manufacturer's marketing. | Test real stream sizes, camera flips, permissions, focus and finalization across vendors. |
| [FIMO official site](https://fimo.app/) presents a vintage-film camera experience and Android download. The indexed page is old; current distribution details were not established. | A small creative vocabulary is a useful design direction, not evidence for a specific current feature set. | Eight original looks; no copying film-stock names, assets, proprietary profiles or mechanical skeuomorphism. |

This is targeted product research for the new app, not the earlier requested comprehensive competitor audit. No user complaint is treated as a repeated market pattern without corroboration.

## Engineering choices and authoritative references

- **Native Kotlin and Compose:** direct access to official CameraX APIs and native permissions/accessibility. [Compose setup](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/setup) and [CameraX architecture](https://developer.android.com/media/camera/camerax/architecture). This is a project fit decision, not a benchmark claim against Flutter or React Native.
- **CameraX Recorder over a custom muxer:** keep codec negotiation, audio and MP4 production within the maintained library. [Video architecture](https://developer.android.com/media/camera/camerax/video-capture). Finalize is the completion boundary; some errors can leave a useful file. [Finalize reference](https://developer.android.com/reference/androidx/camera/video/VideoRecordEvent.Finalize).
- **Effect routing:** the [CameraX/Media3 introduction](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2024/12/whats-new-in-camerax-140-and-jetpack-compose-support.html), dated 17 December 2024, describes applying surface effects to preview, video and photos. Lensqivo now targets preview alone in photo mode, retaining native ImageCapture, and preview plus video in video mode. Photo edits run after capture. [Media3Effect API](https://developer.android.com/reference/androidx/camera/media3/effect/Media3Effect) and [CameraEffect targets](https://developer.android.com/reference/androidx/camera/core/CameraEffect) document this boundary. The adapter remains `1.0.0-alpha04`; it requires physical-device qualification.
- **Color space:** the [pinned GlEffect contract](https://github.com/androidx/media/blob/1.11.1/libraries/effect/src/main/java/androidx/media3/effect/GlEffect.java) defines linear BT.709 for SDR. Original matrices and beauty blending use that space; JPEG export converts sRGB around the same shader. No logarithmic conversion, HDR metadata or scientific film emulation is claimed.
- **Crop/focus:** [Preview transforms](https://developer.android.com/media/camera/camerax/transform-output) and PreviewView's metering factory replace fixed sensor-size assumptions. A shared ViewPort specifies framing. Front video explicitly uses the [recommended mirror mode](https://developer.android.com/reference/androidx/camera/video/VideoCapture.Builder#setMirrorMode(int)).
- **Sound errors:** [AudioStats](https://developer.android.com/reference/androidx/camera/video/AudioStats) documents source silencing and errors that can continue as silent video; Lensqivo keeps an interruption warning rather than treating permission as proof of audio.
- **Storage/permissions:** [shared media storage](https://developer.android.com/training/data-storage/shared/media), [photo picker](https://developer.android.com/training/data-storage/shared/photopicker), and [camera permission guidance](https://developer.android.com/training/permissions/requesting) support permission-light local capture. [Implicit hardware filtering](https://developer.android.com/guide/topics/manifest/uses-feature-element#implicit) requires explicitly marking rear-camera hardware optional for front-only devices.
- **Versions:** [CameraX releases](https://developer.android.com/jetpack/androidx/releases/camera), [Media3 releases](https://developer.android.com/jetpack/androidx/releases/media3), [Compose BOM](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/bom), [AGP 9.2](https://developer.android.com/build/releases/agp-9-2-0-release-notes), and [Gradle compatibility](https://docs.gradle.org/current/userguide/compatibility.html) informed the pinned build. Debug compilation and strict lint are verified in GitHub CI; minified release builds and physical-camera behavior remain separate gates.
- **Distribution integrity:** official [Gradle Dockerfile](https://github.com/gradle/docker-gradle/blob/master/jdk21-noble/Dockerfile) supplies the 9.8.2 distribution checksum. Wrapper scripts and JAR were retrieved from Gradle's v9.8.2 tag. [Wrapper validation guidance](https://docs.gradle.org/current/userguide/gradle_wrapper.html) informs the CI gate; the local JAR hash alone is not independent signature verification.

## What should come next

| Priority | Improvement | Benefit and cost | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| P0 | Qualify actual photo/video capture | Debug build, lint and synthetic tests establish integration boundaries; physical phones establish capture behavior. | Playable saved clips and native JPEGs; no severe lifecycle/storage failures. |
| P1 | Compare preview and export with test charts; record actual photo sizes | A trustworthy look is more valuable than a large filter catalog. Shared rendering does not guarantee matching tone, resolution or display color. | Neutral chart/skin/color/exposure test across rear/front, both modes, orientations and strengths. |
| P1 | Reliable capability profiles and capture diagnostics | Explain actual size, supported quality and failure reason; test a high-quality path on capable hardware. | No unsupported fps/codec promises, test FHD/HD/SD negotiation and recovery. |
| P1 | Stress the implemented original/pending-media recovery | Source and emulator tests cover retained originals, explicit pending visibility, deduplication and conservative cleanup. Actual process termination/storage exhaustion still need testing. | Kill at every transaction boundary; never delete successful or unrelated media. |
| P2 | Film tone curves and resolution-consistent grain | More convincing texture/highlight response than matrix color alone. Requires custom GPU processing; temporal grain must avoid flicker and color banding. | Linear-light chart validation, multiple export sizes, frame pacing/memory/thermal comparisons. |
| P2 | Non-destructive post-capture editing | Original copies and three reusable recipe slots now exist. An editor still needs media selection, versioned per-photo recipe association and safe re-export. | Full-resolution re-export, durable original/recipe association and malformed-media tests. |
| P2 | Useful manual controls and monitoring | WB lock, focus assist and exposure aids when supported; avoid adding controls that the camera cannot honor. | Camera2 capability validation, metering tests and accessible adjustment UI. |
| P3 | Bounded preset import/export | Offline sharing once look recipes are versioned and stable. Treat files as untrusted, cap byte/entry sizes, reject non-finite values. | Malformed/truncated/oversized import tests and identical re-export rendering. |

Do not start with RAW video, camera hacks that bypass vendor restrictions, background recording, cloud accounts, a social feed, subscriptions or dozens of renamed stock presets. These do not establish the first release's core value: a camera that captures the moment and saves the look the user chose.
